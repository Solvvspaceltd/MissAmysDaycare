Chapter two

A trusted name in Surrey Quays childcare

Miss Amy’s Daycare is a professional, Ofsted-registered early years setting in Surrey Quays, SE16. Miss Amy provides a safe, stimulating and deeply nurturing environment where every child is seen, valued and supported.

Rooted in the EYFS framework, with specialist experience supporting children with additional needs, including non-verbal and limited speech. Miss Amy’s approach is always personal, always intentional.

Her long-term vision is to grow from a highly regarded childminding setting into a fully registered early years prep school raising the standard of early education in the community.

  • EYFS Qualified Practitioner Full Early Years Foundation Stage certification
  • Safeguarding Lead Level 3 trained and regularly updated
  • Paediatric First Aid Current certification held
  • SEN Specialist Non-verbal and additional needs expertise
  • Ofsted Registered DBS cleared, fully compliant setting. URN 2514998

Chapter three

Specialist care for children with additional needs

Miss Amy has extensive experience supporting children with SEN, including those who are non-verbal or have limited speech. Every child with additional needs is supported through a plan written in partnership with their family and, where there is one, their speech therapist or health visitor.

Who we support
  • Non-verbal
  • Limited speech
  • Autism
  • ADHD
  • Developmental delay
  • Down’s syndrome
  • Sensory processing
  • EHCP in place
  • Awaiting assessment
  • Selective mutism
  • Dyspraxia

Chapter five

Common questions answered

If yours isn’t here, message us on WhatsApp and Miss Amy will answer it herself.

View all policies

What ages do you accept?

We welcome babies from 3 months through to school-age children up to 11 years. Different sessions are tailored to each age group.

Are you Ofsted registered?

Yes — fully Ofsted registered. You can use government-funded childcare hours and childcare vouchers where applicable.

Do you accept funded hours?

Yes — we accept 15 and 30 hours government-funded childcare for eligible children. Contact us to check current availability and your eligibility.

Can you support additional needs?

Yes. Miss Amy has extensive experience supporting children with SEN including non-verbal and limited speech. Visit our SEN page to learn more about our approach.

How do I join the waiting list?

Complete our waiting list form online or book a visit to discuss your family’s needs in person. Spaces are allocated as they become available.

What is your safeguarding policy?

All staff hold up-to-date safeguarding training and enhanced DBS checks. Our full safeguarding policy is available to download on the Policies page.
